Connect Renpho to Claude: your options

Each Renpho MCP server and bridge can put Renpho scale data in front of an AI assistant. Here is how they differ, checked against their public pages on 2026-11-12. Projects change, so verify before you choose.

Servers that log in to Renpho

ProjectHow it runsWhere your password goes
StartupBros/renpho-mcp-serverNode.js 18+, clone and build locally. Body composition, trends, history, sync diagnostics. MIT.Environment variable on your machine, sent to Renpho.
jegork/renpho-mcpNode.js, run with npx or a single-file bundle. Read-only. MIT.Environment variable on your machine, sent to Renpho.
a1dancole/renpho-mcpRemote server on Cloudflare Workers, works on Claude web, desktop and mobile. Trend and goal tools. MIT.Kept server-side, encrypted in its OAuth grant, so it can refresh sessions (per its README).
Renpho MCP Server (this project)Docker image run over stdio, read-only, includes tape-measure data. MIT.Environment variable in your container, sent to Renpho by the SDK.

Routes that do not use your Renpho password

Apple Health or Health Connect. The Renpho Health app can write readings to Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). Hosted services such as Wellness Project read that data and serve it to Claude or ChatGPT, and local Apple Health MCP servers exist as well. You avoid sharing the Renpho login, but your data goes through a hub and, for hosted services, a third party.

CSV export. The renpho-api command-line tool can save your history as JSON and CSV, which you can upload to an assistant by hand.

Which should you pick?

  • You want a container and no Node toolchain on your machine, or you also use the tape measure: this project.
  • You want a Node server with the most features, or the biggest user base among Renpho servers: StartupBros.
  • You want an npx one-liner: jegork.
  • You want it inside Claude on your phone and accept a hosted component: a1dancole or a hosted bridge.
  • You do not want to share your Renpho password with any tool: the Apple Health or Health Connect route.

All direct servers rely on the same unofficial Renpho API, so any of them can break when Renpho changes it.
